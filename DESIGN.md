## Brand & Style

The design system projects an elevated, cyber-luxe hospitality identity for on-demand garment care. It deliberately breaks away from the clinical whites and pastel blues typical of utility laundromats, positioning fabric care as high-touch personal luxury powered by real-time technology. 

The aesthetic fuses **Dark Neo-Glow Minimalism** with **Glassmorphism**: deep, atmospheric obsidian-indigo grounds layered with radiant neon magenta, violet, and electric rose accents. Surfaces rely on semi-translucent backdrops, diffused atmospheric backlighting, and crisp gradient borders mimicking luminescence. The emotional response is modern, effortless, meticulous, and prestige-driven—assuring clients their garments are treated with bespoke care.

## Colors

The palette establishes an electric nocturnal atmosphere, anchoring deep jewel tones with striking high-luminescence accents:

- **Canvas & Base Surface (`#191825`)**: The core dark ground, suppressing eye fatigue while making vibrant neon elements hover vividly.
- **Deep Indigo Container (`#060047`)**: Structural card backdrops, nav rails, and floating surfaces. Blended with low opacities to produce frosted dark glass.
- **Primary Violet (`#865DFF`)**: Main CTAs, selected tab indicators, active state highlights, and primary brand marks.
- **Light Neon Violet (`#E384FF`)**: Interactive hover and active states for primary controls, plus outer boundary glows.
- **Soft Electric Pink (`#FFA3FD`)**: Informational tags, status chips, service badges, and secondary typographic emphasis.
- **Rose Pink (`#FF5F9E`)**: Success states, completed timeline pips, metric counters, and feature highlights.
- **Hot Pink (`#B3005E`)**: Attention-demanding warnings, limited-time express service notices, and secondary emphasis.
- **Vivid Magenta (`#E90064`)**: Critical alerts, error confirmations, and high-energy gradient stops.
- **Text Hierarchy**: Headings sit in Pure Starlight (`#FFFFFF`), secondary body in Lavender Mist (`#C5C3DF`), and subdued captions in Muted Slate (`#7E7B9A`).

## Typography

Typography is set entirely in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, contemporary proportions, and generous apertures that maintain pristine legibility against saturated dark fields.

- **Scale & Contrast**: Hero headlines leverage ultra-tight tracking (`-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`800`) to create confident editorial impact. Body copy uses relaxed letter-spacing and open line-heights to guarantee scanning comfort across low-light environments.
- **Micro-Copy & Tags**: The `label-caps` token requires uppercase casing for super-titles, order statuses, and tier callouts, pairing intense neon hues with structural weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system employs a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a maximum container boundary of `1280px` on desktop, centering dynamic interactive elements against an infinite dark field.

- **Breakpoints**: 
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` screen margins)
  - Tablet: `768px - 1024px` (8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2.5rem` screen margins)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `4rem` screen margins)
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows a 4px-aligned scale (`space-xs` to `space-xl`). Vertical sections on the landing page maintain wide intervals (`space-xl` scaled up to `6rem` via section paddings) to allow neon gradient bleeds and translucent card boundaries room to breathe without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to **Roundedness 2 (Rounded)**, creating balanced, human-friendly hardware and digital product harmony:

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) delivering defined silhouettes with curved boundaries.
- **Modals, Showcase Displays, & Large Feature Sections**: `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`).
- **Input Fields, Tooltips, & Service Category Tiles**: `rounded` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- **Buttons, Status Badges, & Counter Pips**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to contrast geometrically against rectangular pricing structures and tracker matrices.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#865DFF` background with pure white text (`label-lg`). Hover triggers a smooth shift to `#E384FF` alongside an active outer aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(227, 132, 255, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary Ghost / Border**: Pill-shaped, transparent background with a `1.5px` border gradient transitioning from `#865DFF` to `#FFA3FD`. Hover fills with `rgba(134, 93, 255, 0.15)`.

### Badges & Chips
- Status tags and category badges use `label-caps` in a pill container. 
- Background is set to a 10% tint of the badge state color (e.g., `#FFA3FD` for info, `#FF5F9E` for success, `#B3005E` for warnings) bounded by a matching `1px` hairline stroke at 30% opacity.

### Pricing Cards
- Structured using Layer 1 glass containers.
- **Featured / Most Popular Plan**: Highlighted by a multi-stop perimeter gradient border (`linear-gradient(135deg, #865DFF, #FFA3FD, #E90064)`), an absolute-positioned floating pill badge over the top edge, and a soft interior radial glow.

### Order Tracking Preview
- Interactive horizontal or vertical timeline featuring glowing step nodes.
- Completed stages connect via solid `#FF5F9E` neon lines; current active stage pulses with `#865DFF` glow; upcoming stages use dimmed `#7E7B9A` lines with 20% opacity.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Set against `#060047` at 50% opacity, bordered by `1px solid rgba(197, 195, 223, 0.15)`. Text typed in `#FFFFFF`.
- On `:focus`, the border transitions to `#865DFF` with a subtle field glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(134, 93, 255, 0.25)`).

### Customer Reviews & Testimonials
- Cards feature frosted dark indigo glass, star ratings in neon `#FFA3FD`, customer avatar rings bordered in `#865DFF`, and quote copy in muted `#C5C3DF`.
